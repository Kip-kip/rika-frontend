Quick answer

For most Kenyan homes, aluminium is the better long-term choice. It costs slightly more upfront (typically 10–20%) but lasts longer, needs less maintenance, handles the climate better, and offers superior security. UPVC makes sense for tight budgets or where white-only finish is acceptable.

Which should you pick?

Recommended for most homes

Choose Aluminium if…

  • You want a modern, slim frame with a range of colours
  • You're in an area with high UV exposure (sunny parts of Nairobi, Kiambu, Ruiru)
  • Security is a priority — you want the strongest frame
  • You want 25+ years of service with minimal maintenance
  • You're doing a premium/comfort tier and want the best finish
Budget option

Choose UPVC if…

  • You're working to a strict budget and need the lowest upfront cost
  • White-only finish is acceptable for your project
  • You're in a cooler, shadier location with lower UV exposure
  • Thermal insulation is your top priority (e.g. a cold region)
  • You're doing a short-term rental or temporary structure

FAQ

Is aluminium more expensive than UPVC in Kenya?

At the same spec, aluminium windows in Kenya are typically 10–20% more expensive than UPVC due to the metal frame cost. However, aluminium lasts longer (25–40 years vs 15–25 for UPVC) and requires less maintenance, so the total cost of ownership is often comparable or lower over a 20-year period.

Which is better for Kenya's climate?

Aluminium is the better long-term choice for Kenya's climate. It handles UV, rain, and dust better than UPVC, which can become brittle and discolour over time. Aluminium also resists termites and pests that affect wood, and doesn't warp in high heat the way some UPVC profiles do.

Can I repaint an aluminium window?

Yes. Aluminium frames are powder-coated in a range of finishes (matte black, silver, bronze, white, or custom RAL). If the coating is damaged, the frame can be re-powder-coated. UPVC is typically supplied in white only and cannot be repainted without specialist coatings.

Which gives better security?

Aluminium frames are structurally stronger and harder to force. Combined with anti-burglar mesh (available on Rika's Premium tier), aluminium windows offer superior security. UPVC frames are lighter and can be more vulnerable to forced entry unless reinforced with steel intruder protection.
