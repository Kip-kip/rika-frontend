Built for you

Engineered for every part of your home

Light, ventilation & a view worth keeping

Large sliding or hinged openings in powder-coated aluminium frames, with clear or low-iron glass for maximum light. Add a burglar grill on the ground floor without losing the clean look. Your living room, but better lit and better sealed.

🛋️

Quiet, private, secure

Casement windows with frosted or tinted glass for privacy, tight weather seals for quiet, and concealed hardware. Ground-floor bedrooms get anti-burglar grills as standard on our Comfort and Premium tiers.

🛏️

Frosted glass, zero mould worries

Fixed or top-hinged openings with frosted glass — full privacy, no grubby screens. Aluminium never swells in steam like uPVC, and the finish wipes clean in seconds.

🚿

Shopfronts that draw the eye

Floor-to-ceiling glass shopfronts, partition walls, and sliding doors built for foot traffic and security. Low-iron glass keeps your display bright; anti-theft hardware keeps you open.
