Frequently asked in Ruiru

Do you deliver in Ruiru?

Yes. Ruiru, Ruiru CBD, Ruiru industrial area, and the greater Ruiru / Kiambu corridor. Delivery within Nairobi is free; across the wider corridor a small transport fee applies and is stated up front.

Is the measurement really free?

Yes — free, no obligation. A technician measures on site and you get a firm price the same day.

What's your warranty?

A written workmanship warranty from 1 to 10 years depending on the tier you pick. It's in writing, not a verbal promise.

How fast can you fit?

Most Ruiru orders are measured, made and fitted within the same week to two weeks, depending on size.
