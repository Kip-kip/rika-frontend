Frequently asked
What's the difference between a sliding and a casement door?

A sliding door runs on a top-and-bottom track and saves floor space — ideal for patios and wide openings. A casement door hinges on one side and is better for entry doors where you want a solid, secure, weather-tight closure.

Can aluminium doors be burglar-proof?

Yes. We fit multi-point locking as standard and offer anti-burglar grills and laminated glass as add-ons. Laminated glass is also your best choice if you want added privacy or a tinted look.

How much do aluminium doors cost in Kenya?

Sliding door systems start around KSh 11,000 per m², casement doors around KSh 13,500 per m². Fixed glazing beside a door starts around KSh 8,500 per m². The calculator gives you a range in seconds; the firm figure comes at the free measurement.
