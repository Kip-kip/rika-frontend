☀️ Solar control

Nairobi sun is strong on west- and north-facing walls. Tinted glass can cut solar heat gain and glare without blacking out the room — a real comfort and energy saving.

👁️ Privacy without losing light

Frosted glass lets the day in while hiding what's inside. Our pick for bathrooms, bedrooms, and any window that looks onto a neighbour's property.

🛡️ Security glazing

Laminated glass is the first line of defence at entry points. On impact it holds together, which slows a break-in and reduces the risk of sharp shards.

Frequently asked
What's the difference between tinted and frosted glass?

Tinted glass is still clear — it just has a colour (light or dark) that reduces glare and heat. Frosted glass is diffused, so you can't see through it at all. Tint = privacy from glare; frosted = privacy from sight.

Is laminated glass worth it?

For entry doors and ground-floor windows, yes. It's the most effective single upgrade for security. For upper-floor rooms where the main concern is light or privacy, clear or tinted is usually enough.

How much does tinted or frosted glass add to a window?

Light or dark tint is typically KSh 1,500 per window, and frosted is about KSh 2,500 per window, on top of the base per-m² frame price. Laminated glass is quoted at measurement since it depends on thickness and size.
