Frequently asked
Can a partition replace a solid wall?

For room dividers and feature walls, yes. For load-bearing walls or where structural support is needed, we'll flag it at the survey and recommend a hybrid approach (glass plus a structural frame).

What glass do you recommend for offices?

Frosted or laminated. Frosted gives privacy without losing light; laminated is the safer choice for high-traffic areas because it won't shatter clean on impact.

How much do aluminium partitions cost in Kenya?

Fixed glass panels start around KSh 8,500 per m², sliding partitions around KSh 11,000 per m², and hinged systems around KSh 13,500 per m². The calculator gives you a range; the firm figure comes at the free measurement.
