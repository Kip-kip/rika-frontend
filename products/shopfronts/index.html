Frequently asked
Can you make a shopfront that's both see-through and secure?

Yes. Laminated (safety) glass keeps the see-through look but won't shatter clean on impact, and we can add anti-burglar mesh or grills behind it. It's the most common commercial combination we install.

How do you price a shopfront?

Per m² of glazing, using the same per-m² rates as our windows and doors, plus add-ons for doors, grills and laminated glass. The calculator gives you a range; the firm figure comes at the free on-site survey.

Do you install outside of business hours?

Yes — for shops we can schedule installation outside your trading hours or over a weekend so you don't lose business. We confirm the timing at the survey.
