Measure Your Opening

Get an accurate measurement of your window opening from a photo. Place an A4 sheet of paper against the opening, take a photo, and we'll calculate the real dimensions.

💡 Tips for an accurate photo:
  • Place an A4 sheet (210 × 297 mm) flat inside the opening
  • Make sure the full paper is visible and not folded
  • Stand back ~1–2 m, keep the camera level with the opening
  • Good even lighting, avoid glare on the paper
